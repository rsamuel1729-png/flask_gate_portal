Import questions from PDF or Word

Upload a text-based PDF or a .docx document. Select the subject and an optional default topic. The importer extracts numbered MCQs, shows a preview, then shuffles the accepted questions into batches of 30. A 90-question upload becomes three sets; a 40-question upload becomes a 30-question set plus a 10-question carryover set.

For reliable extraction, format each question as shown in the question document example. Include all four options and an explicit answer. The importer previews what it finds before saving; scanned image-only PDFs need OCR before upload. Max 20 MB per file.
